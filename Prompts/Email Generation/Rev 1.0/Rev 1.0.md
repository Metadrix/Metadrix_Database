**Context:**  
I (the sender) am part of a collective of students from BITS Pilani with over 2 years of individual freelancing experience in UI/UX and app/web dev. We offer top-tier, modern, animated websites at affordable rates, disrupting a market filled with expensive, sub-optimal agencies.  
The Target Client is a successful real estate firm with strong offline revenue but a poor or failed online presence.

**Input Data:**

1. **Client Data (.md file):** Contains their current details, pain points, and lack of strategy.
    
2. **Competitor Data (.md file):** Contains examples of successful competitors in their niche.
    

**Task:**  
Analyze the provided .md files to understand the client's specific business and how they are falling behind their competition. Then, write a 300-400 word email following the structure below.

**Email Structure:**

1. **Subject Line:** Create 3 options: professional, urgent, and value-driven.
    
2. **The Hook (Goodwill & Alignment):** Acknowledge their offline success and reputation. Immediately mention that we have performed a **free, preliminary SEO/SEM & Site Audit** as a gesture of goodwill because we see their potential. Mention that the full report screenshots are attached.
    
3. **The "Gap" (Competitor Comparison):** Using the Competitor .md file, contrast their current online state with their competitors. Use an urgent tone ("You are falling behind") to explain that while competitors are using modern funnels to capture new-gen wealthy clients, this client is losing that market share due to their current website and strategy.
    
4. **The Problem:** Briefly highlight the specific shortcomings of their current website (based on Client .md) and how this creates a lack of trust for digital-first customers.
    
5. **Who We Are (The Solution):** Introduce us as "[Company Name Placeholder], a collective of BITS Pilani designers and developers." Emphasize that we fix these exact problems with high-end, interactive experiences for a fraction of big agency costs.
    
6. **Portfolio Call-Out:** Create a distinct, centered section for our portfolio link.
    
7. **Call to Action:** Do not ask for a project yet. Ask for a **5-minute call** to walk them through the audit results and show them exactly how to stop losing leads to competitors.
    

**Tone & Style Guidelines:**

- **Tone:** Soft but convincing (4/10 intensity). Consultative yet urgent regarding competitor dominance.
    
- **Length:** 300-400 words strict.
    
- **Format:** concise paragraphs, clear flow.
    
- **No Fluff:** Do not list generic SEO definitions. Focus on _lost revenue_ and _brand perception_.
